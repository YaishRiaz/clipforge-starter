# Code of Conduct

ClipForge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below does not replace it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: clear issues, focused pull requests.
- Protect others' privacy. Never post someone's keys, accounts, channel details or personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using the project's spaces to promote breaking platform rules, such as fake engagement, account farming or detection evasion.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private vulnerability / contact options](SECURITY.md) or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
