# Clip SOP: making clips people watch

The working checklist for every clip. Where a campaign brief disagrees, the brief wins; the compliance check enforces it.

## 1. Hook (first 3 seconds)
- The first frame carries the hook text, and the clip starts on the moment itself, not on a lead-in.
- Hook must be true to the clip and paid off inside it. No exaggerated hooks, no "wait for it", no rage bait (FM27, Metaphor and Skii briefs ban them).
- Start on the first word of the setup sentence; if it opens with filler ("um", "so", "okay"), start at the next sentence.
- On-screen text hook: short (under 60 characters), high contrast, large, inside the safe area.

## 2. Retention (the middle)
- Cut dead air and filler; keep the clip tight. Under 60 s unless the brief says otherwise; shorter is better when the payoff is early.
- End on the payoff, not after it. A bet, attempt or prediction runs through the result and the reaction.
- Gentle punch-in zooms on emphasis are fine when they serve the moment. Never crop out required overlays (FM27 logo, handle).
- Captions: word-timed, two lines max, high contrast, clear of the Shorts UI. Skip them when the footage already has burned-in subs.

## 3. Audio
- Original audio stays clear. No added music on FM27 or Skii (banned); Metaphor bans music that drowns the original audio. If a brief allows music, keep it light and under the speech.
- Light sound effects only where the brief does not ban them.

## 4. Added value (reused content)
- Every clip carries something of ours: hook text, context captions, a reason-to-watch title, framing. Never post a bare cut.
- The goal is a better clip for viewers, not changing it to avoid platform checks. We do not try to evade detection.

## 5. Distribution
- YouTube Shorts is automated. TikTok and Instagram Reels are free and by hand: Telegram sends the video and a caption per allowed platform after the YouTube post; Yaish posts from his own account and submits the link.
- Space uploads out. Slots are about 2.5-3 h apart and the minimum gap is 2 h; never two clips of the same moment.
- Each trailer video is posted at most 5 times per campaign. Never reuse footage across campaigns.
- Required text per brief (hashtags, handles, #Ad line) goes in every platform's caption.

## 6. Review
- Nothing posts without Yaish's approval while in review mode. Reject framing, doubled captions or banned edits and note why.
