# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

ClipForge runs entirely on your own computer. The ClipForge project has no servers, does not
collect analytics, and receives no data from you.

## What ClipForge accesses

When you connect your TikTok account, ClipForge asks TikTok for these permissions only:

- **user.info.basic**: your TikTok display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
- **video.upload**: to send clips you approved to your TikTok drafts (inbox).

It does not read your messages, followers, contacts or other videos, and it cannot publish on
your behalf; you post each draft yourself in the TikTok app.

YouTube access works the same way: only what is needed to upload your approved clips and read
their view counts.

## Where your data is kept

- Access tokens are stored only on your computer, in ClipForge's local data folder, and are
  never sent anywhere except to TikTok or Google when ClipForge calls their official APIs.
- Videos, transcripts and clip data stay on your computer.
- Some features send clip transcripts to the AI provider you configure (for example Anthropic)
  to choose moments and write titles. No TikTok account data is sent to them.

## Sharing

ClipForge's authors never receive, sell or share your data.

## Removing access

Disconnect ClipForge at any time in the TikTok app: **Settings and privacy → Security →
Apps and services (Manage app permissions)**, then remove ClipForge. Deleting ClipForge's data
folder removes everything it stored.
